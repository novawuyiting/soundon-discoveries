What is a vibe coding playlist?

A vibe coding playlist is music for programming by feel: staying in motion, prompting AI tools, testing ideas quickly, and keeping enough focus to finish. This playlist is tuned for builders who want coding music that feels modern, calm, and slightly futuristic without turning into background noise.

Use it for AI app prototypes, website builds, debugging, product sprints, study sessions, and focused creative work.
