What makes a good AI coding playlist?

A good AI coding playlist gives you momentum without asking for too much attention. When you are coding with AI tools, the rhythm changes: you prompt, wait, scan output, test, reject, fix, and keep moving. The music needs enough energy to keep the session alive, but not so much that it competes with the code.

This playlist is tuned for AI app prototypes, Cursor sessions, Codex tasks, website builds, debugging loops, and deep work blocks where you want a modern coding soundtrack rather than generic background noise.
