What is Brazilian phonk?

Brazilian phonk blends phonk energy with Brazilian funk and funk automotivo influence, often built for edits, cars, gym clips, and fast replay.

What is montagem phonk?

Montagem phonk is a high-motion lane around montagem-style tracks, quick hooks, and aggressive rhythms that work well in short-form videos.
